## Project background

This project enhances an existing coffee-shop website for customers ordering drinks on desktop, tablet, and mobile. The goal is to preserve the supplied brand, navigation, product presentation, animations, and visual identity while adding a complete browser-only ordering experience.

## Product shape

- Static bilingual English/Khmer coffee-shop storefront.
- All 43 supplied drinks retain their original images, English/Khmer content, category placement, and base prices.
- Product cards open one reusable configurable drink modal with live pricing for size, sugar, ice, add-ons, and quantity.
- A localStorage-backed cart supports quantity changes, removal, totals, and checkout.
- Checkout collects non-sensitive customer details and offers cash, card demo, or replaceable QR payment.
- Successful orders receive date-based sequential local order numbers, a success view, and printable/downloadable HTML receipt.
- The original hero, animated café ribbon, celebrations, reviews, footer, brand colors, fonts, and supporting pages remain represented.
- Privacy and Terms are fully styled responsive legal pages with shared bilingual navigation and localStorage language preference.
- No backend, database, authentication, or real payment processing.
- Accessibility, responsive behavior, keyboard controls, and reduced-motion support are required.
